# Construction Company Website — Design Specification

## 1. Design Direction

Create a premium, modern commercial-construction website inspired by the supplied reference image.

**Overall style**
- Corporate construction / architecture aesthetic
- Clean, professional and trustworthy
- Strong editorial typography
- Large architectural imagery
- White/light-gray sections balanced with dark navy and red accents
- Geometric background patterns in selected sections
- Spacious layouts with strong visual hierarchy
- Subtle hover effects and smooth scrolling animations
- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile

**Visual mood**
- Premium construction company
- Architectural / engineering focused
- Reliable, established and professional
- High-end but not overly flashy

---

## 2. Color Palette

Use approximately:

- **Primary Red:** `#E3261A`
- **Dark Navy:** `#17222C`
- **Charcoal:** `#25292D`
- **White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Light Gray:** `#F4F5F6`
- **Text Gray:** `#666666`
- **Border Gray:** `#E5E5E5`

Red should be used for primary CTAs, active states, icons, highlights and small visual accents.

---

## 3. Typography

Use a bold condensed/display font for headings and a clean sans-serif for body copy.

Suggested combination:
- Headings: **Oswald / Bebas Neue / Roboto Condensed**
- Body: **Roboto / Open Sans / Inter**

Headings should generally be:
- Uppercase
- Bold
- Tight line-height
- Strong visual presence

Examples:
- `BUILD. INVEST. INNOVATE.`
- `HIGH QUALITY CONSTRUCTION SOLUTIONS`
- `RECENTLY COMPLETED WORKS`
- `WHAT OUR CLIENT SAY`

---

Left:

Center/right:

- ABOUT US

Far right:

# 6. Services Section

Background:
- Very light gray/white
- Subtle geometric hexagon/cube pattern in the background

Centered eyebrow:
`OUR SERVICES`

Large heading:
`HIGH QUALITY CONSTRUCTION SOLUTIONS FOR RESIDENTIAL & INDUSTRIES!`

Create a 3-column grid on desktop and 2/1 columns on smaller screens.

### Service Cards

Cards should be clean white rectangles with subtle shadow/border.

Each card contains:
1. Red line-art construction icon
2. Service title
3. Short description
4. Small dark/black square arrow button

Services:

### New Construction
Construction of new residential, commercial and industrial properties from planning through completion.

### Design-Build
Integrated architectural design and construction services delivered through one coordinated team.

### Remodel & Renovations
Professional renovation and remodeling solutions for existing residential and commercial spaces.

### Interior Design
Modern interior planning, material selection and finishing solutions.

### Site Improvements
Site preparation, exterior improvements, infrastructure and development services.

### Construction Consult
Professional construction consultation, planning and project guidance.

### Interaction
- First card can use red background with white text as the featured/active state.
- Other cards use white backgrounds.
- Hovering a card should transition toward the red featured style.
- Arrow button can slide slightly on hover.

# 14. Responsive Design

## Desktop
- Full navigation
- 3-column service cards
- 3-column project/blog grids
- Two-column about and enquiry sections
- Horizontal statistics

## Tablet
- Navigation may reduce spacing
- Services become 2 columns
- Projects become 2 columns
- Blog becomes 2 columns
- Statistics remain horizontal where possible

## Mobile
- Hamburger navigation
- Hero typography scales down
- Services become one column
- Projects become one column
- Testimonials become one card at a time
- Statistics stack or use a 2x2 grid
- Blog becomes one column
- About section stacks image above text
- Enquiry form becomes single column
- Footer columns stack vertically

---

# 15. Animation & Interaction

Keep animations professional and subtle.

Implement:
- Smooth page scrolling
- Fade-up reveal on section entry
- Image zoom on project/blog hover
- Button hover transitions
- Service card hover transitions
- Animated counters
- Testimonial carousel
- Project carousel
- Blog carousel
- Sticky header transition
- Mobile menu slide/fade animation

Avoid excessive animations that make the website feel like a template.

---

# 16. Image Direction

Use realistic, premium construction photography.

Preferred imagery:
- Modern commercial buildings
- Architects and engineers
- Construction workers wearing safety gear
- Cranes and active construction sites
- Modern interiors
- Industrial architecture
- Completed commercial projects

Images should feel cohesive:
- Neutral/cool architectural tones
- Strong geometry
- Professional photography
- High resolution

Do not use cartoonish stock illustrations.

---

# 18. UX Requirements

- Every CTA should have a clear hover/focus state.
- Buttons must have readable contrast.
- Form fields require visible labels or accessible placeholders.
- Images require meaningful alt text.
- Navigation must be keyboard accessible.
- Maintain consistent spacing between sections.
- Keep important contact/quote CTA visible without being intrusive.
- Optimize image loading and use lazy loading for below-the-fold images.

---

# 19. Final Visual Target

The final website should visually communicate:

**Trust + Engineering + Premium Architecture + Construction Expertise**

The reference layout should be treated as the visual direction, not as a requirement to copy every exact image or text element.

Maintain the same overall composition:
1. Utility bar
2. White navigation
3. Large architectural hero
4. Services grid
5. About section
6. Dark projects section
7. Testimonials
8. Red statistics strip
9. Blog/news
10. Construction enquiry form
11. Dark multi-column footer

The final result should look like a polished, production-ready construction company website rather than a basic landing page.
